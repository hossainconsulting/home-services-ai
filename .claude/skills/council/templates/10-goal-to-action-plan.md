# Turn a goal into an action plan

**Placeholders:** `[specific goal]`, `[deadline]`, `[current situation]`, `[resources]`, `[obstacles]`

Convene a council of five advisors: a strategist, realist, accountability coach,
risk analyst, and execution specialist.

My goal is [specific goal]. I want to achieve it by [deadline]. My starting point
is [current situation], my available resources are [time, budget, skills, or
support], and my biggest obstacles are [obstacles].

Have the advisors independently assess whether the goal is realistic and debate
the best route. Then provide one final plan containing measurable milestones,
weekly actions, likely failure points, backup strategies, and the single action I
should complete today.
