# Build a content strategy

**Placeholders:** `[topic]`, `[audience]`, `[platform]`, `[goal]`

Create a five-person content council consisting of a social media strategist,
audience psychologist, storytelling expert, analytics specialist, and honest
critic.

I create content about [topic] for [audience] on [platform]. My goal is
[followers, engagement, leads, sales, or authority]. Ask each advisor to identify
what I should post, what I should stop doing, and what would make my content
distinctive.

Then produce a final 30-day strategy with three content pillars, ten specific
post ideas, a realistic posting schedule, and three metrics to track.
