# Validate a business idea

**Placeholders:** `[idea]`, `[target customer]`

Create a council of five advisors: a market researcher, skeptical investor, ideal
customer, growth strategist, and operations expert. Evaluate my business idea:
[describe your idea] for [target customer].

Each advisor must independently explain the biggest opportunity, weakest
assumption, likely obstacle, and one improvement. Let them challenge one
another's conclusions. Finish with a chairman's verdict containing:

- A score out of 10
- The strongest reason to pursue it
- The biggest reason it could fail
- Three assumptions I must test
- A low-cost seven-day validation plan
