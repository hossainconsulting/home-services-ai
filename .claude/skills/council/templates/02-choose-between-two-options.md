# Choose between two options

**Placeholders:** `[option A]`, `[option B]`, `[goal]`, `[constraints]`

Convene five advisors — a practical planner, financial analyst, risk expert,
long-term strategist, and contrarian — to help me choose between [option A] and
[option B].

My goal is [goal]. My constraints are [budget, time, responsibilities, or other
limitations]. Have every advisor compare the options using cost, risk, effort,
short-term benefits, and long-term benefits. Encourage disagreement.

Conclude with one recommendation, the conditions that would change it, and the
first three actions I should take.
