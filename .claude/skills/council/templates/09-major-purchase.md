# Evaluate a major purchase

**Placeholders:** `[product]`, `[purpose]`, `[price]`, `[budget]`, `[alternatives]`

Create a buying council with a budget advisor, product expert, skeptical
reviewer, long-term value analyst, and practical user.

Help me decide whether to buy [product] for [purpose]. It costs [price], my
budget is [budget], and the alternatives I'm considering are [alternatives].

Have each advisor evaluate necessity, value, durability, hidden costs, and
cheaper substitutes. Finish with a buy, wait, or skip verdict; the strongest
argument against that verdict; questions I should ask before purchasing; and the
maximum price worth paying.
