# Make a career decision

**Placeholders:** `[decision]`, `[background]`, `[priorities]`, `[constraints]`

Create a career council consisting of a career coach, hiring manager, financial
realist, future-self advisor, and devil's advocate.

Help me evaluate this career decision: [describe the decision]. My experience is
[background], my priorities are [income, flexibility, growth, stability, purpose,
etc.], and my constraints are [constraints].

Have the advisors discuss opportunities, tradeoffs, hidden risks, and what
information is missing. End with a recommended direction, a decision matrix,
three questions I must answer, and a 30-day action plan.
