# Improve an offer

**Placeholders:** `[product/service]`, `[price]`, `[audience]`, `[promised result]`

Convene five advisors: a customer researcher, conversion copywriter, pricing
strategist, skeptical buyer, and sales expert.

Analyze this offer: [describe your product/service, price, audience, and
promised result]. Each advisor should identify one reason customers would buy,
one reason they would hesitate, and one concrete improvement.

After their debate, rewrite my offer with a clearer promise, stronger value
proposition, improved deliverables, risk-reversal idea, and concise call to
action. Do not invent results, testimonials, or guarantees.
