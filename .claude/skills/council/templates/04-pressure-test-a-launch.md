# Pressure-test a product launch

**Placeholders:** `[product/service]`, `[target audience]`, `[price]`, `[date]`, `[launch plan]`

Form a council containing a product marketer, ideal customer, launch manager,
competitor analyst, and pre-mortem expert.

Review my planned launch for [product/service], intended for [target audience],
priced at [price], and launching on [date]. My current launch plan is [briefly
describe it].

Let the advisors debate whether the offer, pricing, positioning, timing, and
promotion plan are strong enough. Finish with a launch-readiness score, the five
biggest risks, solutions for each risk, and a prioritized launch checklist.
